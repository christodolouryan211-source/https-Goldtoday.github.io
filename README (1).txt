GOLDTODAY WEBSITE
=================

1. Upload index.html to the root of your goldtoday.github.io repository.
2. Keep the repository public.
3. GitHub Pages: Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root).

LIVE DATA
---------
The page contains a safe placeholder market-data layer. Before showing real-time prices,
connect a market-data provider whose license permits website redistribution/display.
Do not scrape or redistribute data without permission.

GOOGLE ADSENSE
--------------
After your site is live, create/connect your AdSense site and paste Google's exact code
into the <head> of index.html. Google reviews the entire site before ads can appear.

CONTACT
-------
Replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with your real email address.

The site is intentionally not configured for synthetic-index/gambling products.
